#Encounters

obj_creature_spots are set up in the room (5 each side)


obj_end_turn
- spawns when the user has a turn, clicking it will end your turn and pass it to the enemy
- draws a new hand of cards

obj_end_box
- spawns at the end of the encounter (displays win and loss data)

obj_confirm
- spawns at the end of the encounter, clicking it will start the transition to the overworld
